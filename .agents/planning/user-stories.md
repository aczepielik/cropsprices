# User Stories

## Primary

As a consumer I want to know what phase of the crops cycle we are currently at and how does it shape prices.

Example decisions I'm making based on these data: 

- I saw that strawberries are more expensive than I'm used to so I initially thought about postponing larger buys. But comparing price dynamics YtY I see that we are close to the peak of the season and strawberries won't be much cheaper this year so I decided to buy.
- I saw good offer for imported fruits but knowing the season just begins I was reluctant to buy now. But I've checked that for imported fruits seasonal effects have minimal impact on prices I decided to buy.

## Secondary

As a farmer I want to track recent price changes so I don't sell biggest bulks neither too early nor too late.

As storage manager I want to know how did price shape in transition period between yester-year and current year to plan the strategy. 

As potential investor I want to know differences between prices of same goods on various markets
